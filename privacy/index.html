Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Fundstash is a personal finance app for Android and iPhone. It is built so that your financial data stays on your phone. There is no Fundstash account and no Fundstash server that receives your transactions, messages, receipts or questions. This policy explains what the app stores, the few network requests it makes, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal or financial data.
  • There is no sign-up, no login and no advertising.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs of our own.
  • Google’s ML Kit, which the Android app uses to read receipts and to run Gemini Nano, sends Google technical diagnostics (see section 5). They do not include your receipts, messages, entries or questions, are not linked to your identity, and are not used for advertising or tracking.
  • Everything you enter or import is stored in an encrypted database on your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone, in an SQLite database encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher). The encryption key is generated on the phone and kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore.

  • Transactions, accounts and wallets, budgets, savings goals, debts and IOUs, recurring bills, categories and merchant rules.
  • For entries created from a bank SMS or other message, the original message text, so you can check the entry and the app can re-read it.
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach, and your app settings.

The home-screen widget shows this month’s totals from the same on-device data; it is hidden in Discreet mode.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. “Clear all local data” in Settings deletes it without uninstalling.

3. Permissions and what they are used for

PermissionWhy
SMS (Android only, optional)To read bank and wallet messages on the device and record completed debits and credits. Promotions, OTPs and personal messages are ignored. Message content is never uploaded.
Notification access (Android only, optional)Some banks now send alerts as RCS chats instead of SMS. If you switch on Read bank notifications in Settings, the app reads the title and text of notifications from messaging apps on the phone, only to record bank alerts. What it reads is processed and stored only on your phone and never uploaded.
Camera and photos (optional)To scan receipts. Text recognition runs on the device: Google ML Kit on Android; on iPhone, Apple Vision by default, with ML Kit as an option in Settings. Photos are not uploaded.
Contacts (optional)To pick a friend’s name when splitting a bill. Only the name you pick is used; the app does not read or copy your address book.
Notifications (optional)Local reminders: budget alerts and monthly debt reminders. They are scheduled on the device; no push service is used.
Face ID / fingerprint (optional)To lock the app. Biometric data is handled by the operating system and never seen by the app.

On iPhone, apps cannot read messages. If you set up the optional Shortcuts automation, iOS hands each incoming text to the app on your device, where it is processed the same way.

4. On-device AI

Fundstash can use an on-device language model to categorise entries, read natural-language notes, double-check imported messages and answer questions in “Ask Fundstash”. It uses Apple Intelligence (iPhone) or Gemini Nano through Android AICore where the phone supports them, or a small open model that you can choose to download: SmolLM2 135M (about 145 MB) or Qwen3 0.6B (about 400 MB). All of these run on your phone; your text and questions are not sent to us or to the model providers. On Android, Gemini Nano is reached through Google’s ML Kit, which sends the technical diagnostics described in section 5.

5. Network requests

The app works offline. It only uses the network in these cases:

  • Exchange rates: only when you tap “refresh rates”, the app requests public rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains no personal data.
  • AI model download: only when you tap “Download” for an optional on-device model, the model file is downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Nothing about you or your data is sent; the request only fetches the file.
  • Google ML Kit diagnostics: on Android, the ML Kit libraries used for receipt text recognition and for Gemini Nano send Google technical diagnostics: device model, operating system and app version, performance and error data, and an installation identifier. On iPhone this applies only if you switch receipt scanning to the ML Kit engine. These diagnostics do not contain your receipts, messages, entries or questions, are not linked to your identity, and are not used for advertising or tracking. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Platform models: Google Play services or Apple may download their own on-device text-recognition or AI models as part of the operating system.
  • Links you open, such as this website or a shared split-bill link, open in your browser or another app.

Like any internet request, these providers can see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

6. Backups, exports and sharing

Backups are created only when you ask. A full backup is encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2); without the password it cannot be read, by us or anyone else. CSV exports can optionally be encrypted the same way. You decide where a backup or export goes (for example Files, Drive or email); that service’s terms then apply.

When you share a split bill, the link contains the bill details (description, amount, names you entered). They sit after the “#” in the link, which browsers never send to a web server; the fundstash.app page that shows them decodes them in the reader’s browser, or the app decodes them on the reader’s phone. IOU links work the same way: the details sit after the “#” in the link. No server stores split-bill or IOU details. Anyone who has the link can read it, so share it only with the people involved.

7. This website

fundstash.app is a static website served by Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, fonts or scripts from other companies. Cloudflare processes standard request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site.

8. Children

Fundstash is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

9. Your choices

You can deny or revoke any permission in your phone’s settings, switch off SMS import, notification reading, on-device AI and the assistant in the app, delete the downloaded AI model, and delete all data at any time. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

  • Stop SMS reading on Android: turn off SMS Auto-Sync in Fundstash Settings, or remove the permission in Android Settings → Apps → Fundstash → Permissions → SMS → Don’t allow.
  • Stop reading notifications on Android: turn off Read bank notifications in Fundstash Settings, or open Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications (called Notification access on some phones) → Fundstash and turn it off.
  • Stop message import on iPhone: open the Shortcuts app → Automation, then turn off or delete the Fundstash message automation (swipe left on it and tap Delete).

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
