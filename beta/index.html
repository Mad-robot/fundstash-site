Join the Fundstash beta

Fundstash is in testing before its public launch on the App Store and Google Play. As a tester you get the app early, and what you tell us helps shape version 1.0. Thank you for helping.

iPhone: TestFlight

  1. Install Apple’s free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  2. On your iPhone, open the invite link below and tap Accept, then Install.
Join the beta on TestFlight

Open this link on the iPhone you want to test on.

Android: Google Play testing

Google only lets testers in after the email address of their Google account has been added to the tester list. So the first step is to send us the email address of the Google account you use for the Play Store.

  1. Send us that email address. We’ll add you to the tester list and reply when it’s done.
  2. Signed in with that Google account, open the testing page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Fundstash from Google Play.
Email us your Google account DM @Madrobot_ on X

Send the email address of your Google Play account by email to hello@fundstash.app or as a DM to @Madrobot_ on X.

Please stay in for 14 days

Before a new app can be published to everyone, Google requires it to be tested by at least 12 people who stay opted in for 14 days in a row. Your time in the test really counts, so please:

  • stay in the test for at least 14 days,
  • keep the app installed and open it now and then,
  • don’t leave the test: leaving and joining again restarts the count.

Feedback

Something wrong, confusing or missing? Write to support@fundstash.app, or use the feedback options in TestFlight or Google Play. If a bank SMS was read wrongly, paste the message with the account numbers masked.

Your data in the beta

Testing builds work like the real app: your data stays on your phone. Fundstash never sends us your transactions or messages, so we only see what you choose to share in feedback. See the privacy policy.
