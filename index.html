How it works

Tracking that happens
by itself.

Every spend lands in your ledger without typing, and every number is worked out on the phone.

01 — BANK SMS

Bank texts become transactions.

On Android, debits and credits log themselves the moment they arrive. On iPhone, one Shortcuts automation does it. Offers, recharge ads and OTPs never make it in.

02 — RECEIPTS

Snap a receipt. Done.

Google ML Kit, or Apple Vision on iPhone, reads the bill on the phone. A decision model picks the merchant, total and category.

03 — VOICE

Just say it.

“Chai 40 yesterday”, “salary 85k from Acme”. Amount, payee, category and date fill themselves.

04 — ON-DEVICE AI

AI that never leaves your phone.

It double-checks every imported message, sorts categories and answers “how much on Swiggy last month?”. Built-in AI where your phone has it, a small open model where it doesn’t.

05 — BUDGETS

Know before you overspend.

Category limits suggested from last month’s spending, with local alerts at 80% and 100%. No push server involved.

06 — WIDGETS

Your budget, at a glance.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets on iPhone, home screen widgets on Android. Amounts disappear when Discreet mode is on.

Questions

Good to know

Does Fundstash read my messages?

On Android, with your permission, it reads SMS on the phone to find completed debits and credits. Promotions, OTPs and personal chats are ignored and nothing is uploaded. iPhone apps cannot read messages, so a one-time Shortcuts automation hands bank texts to Fundstash.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Everything is stored in an encrypted database on your phone.

What if I lose or change my phone?

Export an encrypted backup from Settings. It contains everything, including your settings, and only opens with your password. Restore it on the new phone.

Which phones get the on-device AI?

iPhones with Apple Intelligence and Android phones with Gemini Nano use the built-in model. Any other phone can download a small open model (about 400 MB) once. The app works fully without AI too.

Does it work outside India?

Yes. Pick any of 107 currencies as your base and record spending in any of them; exchange rates work offline and refresh only when you ask. Bank-alert import reads amounts in dollars, euros, pounds, rupees and 30 more currencies, and is most thoroughly tuned for Indian banks today. Receipts, voice entry, budgets and the assistant work in any currency.

Is it free?

Yes, free for personal use. There are no ads and your data is never sold, because we never receive it.
